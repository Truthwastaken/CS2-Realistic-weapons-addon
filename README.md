# CS2-Realistic-weapons-addon
A CS2 addon which changes weapons stats.

# What is this?

just for fun, i've made a mod that changes ~~every~~ weapon's stats to match real-life!

# What does this mean?

~~every~~ weapon has had their rate of fire and magazine capacity changed to their real-life counterparts.

for example, the MP7 now has a 40 round magazine and a blistering high rate of fire of 950 rpm!

i've made sure the weapons with changed magazine capacities (MP7,M4A1-S,FAMAS,M249, AWP!, etc) have correct reserve ammo as well (no more 30 + 100 on the MAC-10 or 35 + 90 on the Galil!)

notable changes:   
CZ-75A has 1000rpm and 16 round magazines

M4A1-S has 950rpm, 30 round mags and different handling with and without a suppressor

M4A4 has the same 950 rpm but equips quicker and reloads faster compared to the M4A1, as well as having a slightly higher movement speed

FAMAS has 30 round magazines, 1000 rpm and is slightly more expensive but i could not find a way to make the 3-round burst quicker

  
AK-47 now has 650rpm, slightly higher damage and is 100 dollars cheaper

  
Galil has a rate of fire of  750rpm and 35 + 105 ammo in total

  
AWP has its glorious 10 round magazines returned

M249 has 200 rounds per belt and 200 reserve, 750rpm, increased damage and higher accuracy while crouched

  
MAC-10 has a staggering 1090 RPM but 30 + 90 ammo in total

MP-9 has 1100 RPM

MP7 is more expensive but has 40 round magazines and 950rpm

UMP-45 has 600rpm and higher damage to compensate

all .45 ACP weapons have had their tracers reduced to simulate their subsonic nature.

sawed-off costs $900 and goes on the bomb slot, meaning you can carry 3 weapons as a T!, however, it doesn't refill ammo on the next round. since it has 4 round tubes and 8 reserve ammo it might end up very expensive. upon death, it will not drop, also, it also has the move speed of a glock.

M1014 has double rpm (240) and no full-auto

Nova now has only 4 round tubes

all shotguns have have their spread tightened, armor performance reduced and rate of fire over all increased.

# Where did you get all of this from?

[Counter-Strike: Global Offensive - Internet Movie Firearms Database - Guns in Movies, TV and Video Games](https://www.imfdb.org/wiki/Counter-Strike:_Global_Offensive)

everything is subject to change with more accurate information, and not every weapon is completed yet.

3. launch CS2 with "-addon test2" as a launch option
